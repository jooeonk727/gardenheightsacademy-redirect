Genesis: Creation to Babel

Genesis 1–11 · keyed to the ESV
Level 1 8 Parts 4 Treasure Wisdom boxes per part Includes SAT/TOEFL reading practice

Eight parts carry a child from the seven days of creation through the garden, the flood and the tower of Babel. Every part pairs the story with an English vocabulary and comprehension workbook and one rotating Treasure Wisdom domain — real places, archaeology, ancient daily life, the feasts — so a child comes away knowing the world the passage happened in, not just the plot.

Recommended Bible edition

This workbook is keyed to the ESV® Bible (Crossway) — chapter and verse references, proper names and the vocabulary taught all follow the ESV. Read Genesis 1–11 in your own ESV Bible alongside each part; the workbook retells and asks about the passage but does not reprint Scripture text. On the age of the earth, the length of the creation days and the historicity questions around Genesis 1–11, we state what the text says and note plainly where sincere Christians read it differently.
