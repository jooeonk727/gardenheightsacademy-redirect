Frequently Asked Questions

Which Bible translation do the workbooks use?
Every workbook is keyed to the ESV (English Standard Version). We don't reproduce Scripture in full, so students should read the referenced passage in their own Bible.
Do I need an internet connection to use a workbook?
You can check your answers instantly as you go, and keep working with no internet connection once a page has loaded.
What do I get with a subscription?
A monthly or yearly plan gives unlimited access to every online workbook, on up to 3 devices per account. Monthly plans can be canceled anytime.
Can I buy just the PDF instead of subscribing?
Yes. For a finished volume, you can buy the Student Edition and Teacher's Edition PDFs together as a one-time purchase, and print or use them offline with no subscription.
Is Lokosmos Academy affiliated with a publisher or church?
No. Lokosmos Academy is an independent study resource, not affiliated with or endorsed by Crossway, any Bible publisher, or any particular denomination.
I found an error in a workbook — how do I report it?
Email us above with the part and section you spotted it in, and we'll look into it and fix it.
