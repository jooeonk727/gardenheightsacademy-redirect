Genesis: Jacob to Joseph

Genesis 24–50 · keyed to the ESV

Six adventures carry a child from Isaac finding a wife through Joseph's rise in Egypt and his family's arrival there. Every adventure will pair the story with an English vocabulary and comprehension workbook and one rotating Treasure Wisdom domain — real places, archaeology and ancient daily life. This volume is currently in development; the six adventures below are shown in their planned order.

Recommended Bible edition

Once built, this workbook will be keyed to the ESV® Bible (Crossway), matching Volume 1 — chapter and verse references, proper names and the vocabulary taught will all follow the ESV, and the student will read Genesis 24–50 in their own ESV Bible alongside each part. On matters where sincere Christians read the patriarchal narratives differently, we plan to state what the text says and note plainly where interpretations diverge, following the same approach as Volume 1.
