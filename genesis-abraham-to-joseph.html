Genesis: Abraham to Joseph

Genesis 12–50 · keyed to the ESV
Level 1 10 Adventures 4 Treasure Wisdom boxes per part In development

Ten adventures carry a child from Abram's call to leave home through Joseph's rise in Egypt and his family's arrival there. Every adventure will pair the story with an English vocabulary and comprehension workbook and one rotating Treasure Wisdom domain — real places, archaeology and ancient daily life. This volume is currently in development; the ten adventures below are shown in their planned order.

Recommended Bible edition

Once built, this workbook will be keyed to the ESV® Bible (Crossway), matching Volume 1 — chapter and verse references, proper names and the vocabulary taught will all follow the ESV, and the student will read Genesis 12–50 in their own ESV Bible alongside each part. On matters where sincere Christians read the patriarchal narratives differently, we plan to state what the text says and note plainly where interpretations diverge, following the same approach as Volume 1.
